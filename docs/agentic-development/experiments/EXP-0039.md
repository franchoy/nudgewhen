## Evidence-label contract

This evidence file uses four evidence labels consistently. Each statement
in this file must be classifiable under exactly one of them.

DIRECT OBSERVATION:
A statement directly produced by a tool call performed during the
referenced slice. The slice's retained tool output is the source of
truth, and the statement claims no more than what the tool output
exposed.

RETAINED DIRECT EVIDENCE:
A statement that references evidence retained from an earlier accepted
slice or from the accepted Phase 4 repository-action chronology. The
evidence was directly observed at its original time; the current slice
does not independently re-observe it.

MAINTAINER-SUPPLIED DECISION:
A statement that records a directive, designation, correction, or
refinement supplied by the maintainer during the referenced slice.
The decision itself is not produced by a tool call. The tool calls
only directly observe whether the decision was applied to the working
tree or to an artifact.

INFERENCE:
A statement that is logically derived from directly observed or
retained facts but is not itself the direct output of a tool call.
Successful later behavior may corroborate an inference but does not
promote the inference to direct observation.

A statement must not silently upgrade an INFERENCE, a
MAINTAINER-SUPPLIED DECISION, or a RETAINED DIRECT EVIDENCE reference
into DIRECT OBSERVATION. A statement must not silently downgrade a
DIRECT OBSERVATION into a weaker label.
